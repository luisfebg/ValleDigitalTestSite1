# Valle Digital Design System

## Brand Overview
Valle Digital is a premium, professional digital consulting and technology partner connecting Colombian roots with international consulting and technology experience. The brand balances light cream, deep Valle green, and restrained warm gold, embodying the narrative: "Rooted in clarity. Built for growth."

## Design Principles
1. **Light, Calm & Premium**: 60-70% warm cream/white canvas, 20-25% deep green, 5-10% restrained warm gold accents.
2. **Generous Whitespace & Rhythmic Spacing**: Section padding between 80px to 120px with crisp 8pt rhythm.
3. **Restrained Craft**: Clean cards, 1px soft borders (`#DDE5DF`), subtle shadows, 2px stroke line iconography in Valle Green.
4. **Purposeful Contrast**: Deep green reserved for high-impact CTA banners and footer anchors.
5. **No Agency Buzzwords**: Clear, direct, human tone.

## Color Palette
- **Valle Deep Green**: `#143327` (Primary brand color, dark headers, dark CTA banners)
- **Valle Green**: `#185B45` (Interactive elements, primary icons, active states)
- **Growth Green**: `#277A5B` (Accents & badge highlights)
- **Valle Gold**: `#C8963E` (Main accent, primary buttons, premium badges)
- **Light Gold**: `#E0B75F` (Gradient highlights)
- **Warm Cream**: `#FAF8F2` (Main website canvas & background)
- **Soft Sage**: `#EEF3EF` (Alternate section background)
- **Pure White**: `#FFFFFF` (Cards, elevated containers)
- **Charcoal Green**: `#1D2923` (Body text)
- **Muted Grey Green**: `#66736C` (Secondary text, subtitles)
- **Soft Border**: `#DDE5DF` (Dividers, card borders)

## Typography
- **Headings**: `Manrope`, sans-serif (Weights: 600, 700, 800)
- **Body**: `Inter`, sans-serif (Weights: 400, 500, 600)
- **Scale**:
  - Hero H1: 56px - 64px, tracking -0.02em, line-height 1.15
  - H2: 40px - 44px, tracking -0.015em, line-height 1.25
  - H3: 24px - 28px
  - Card Title: 18px - 20px
  - Hero Body: 18px - 20px
  - Standard Body: 16px - 18px
  - Small / Eyebrow: 13px - 14px, tracking 0.08em, uppercase

## Radii & Elevation
- Buttons: `8px`
- Small Badges & Inputs: `8px` - `10px`
- Cards: `14px` (border: 1px solid `#DDE5DF`, shadow: `0 8px 30px rgba(20, 51, 39, 0.05)`)
- Large Containers & CTA Panels: `20px`

## Key UI Components & Buttons
- **Primary CTA**: Background `#C8963E` (or linear gradient `#E0B75F` to `#C8963E`), text `#143327` (bold, 8px radius).
- **Secondary Button**: Background `#FFFFFF` or transparent, border 1.5px solid `#143327`, text `#143327`.
- **Navigation**: Clean sticky header on Warm Cream (`#FAF8F2`), Logo with palm emblem, links with subtle hover, gold "Talk to us" button.
